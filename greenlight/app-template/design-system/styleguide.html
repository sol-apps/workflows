Greenlight design system

White ground, two typefaces, one working green. Everything on this page comes from tokens.css + components.css — if it isn't here, it isn't in the system. Toggle the theme (◐ above) to check both modes.

Principles

Rules of thumb for building the wizard, review queue, and gateway dashboard on this system.

Colour

Green is the working colour — CTAs, live signals, focus. Tier colours (green / amber / red) are semantic verdicts only; never use them decoratively. Everything else stays white and ink.

Type

Two typefaces: Bricolage Grotesque for headings and numbers with presence, Hanken Grotesk for everything else. Hierarchy comes from size and weight — never letter-spacing, uppercase, or a third font.

Surfaces

--bg is the page, --bg-sunken is wells and inactive segments, --surface is anything raised (cards, rows, panels). Raised things get a border + --shadow-sm, and lift to --shadow-md on hover.

Transparency

Every app shows its tier, owner, scopes, and source. Prefer showing the fact over hiding it behind a click.

Type scale

Two families, seven sizes. Use the tokens; never invent a size. Display sizes always use .t-display (Bricolage). Small labels are heavy, not uppercase.

--text-3xl (36)Hero & auth headings
--text-2xl (28)Page titles, stat numbers
--text-xl (22)Panel & section titles
--text-lg (18)Card titles
--text-base (15)Body copy — Hanken Grotesk carries all prose and descriptions.
--text-sm (13)Secondary text: chips, buttons, table cells, card descriptions.
--text-xs (12)Captions and meta — 312 calls, owners, timestamps. Weight 700 when it's a label.

Buttons

One primary action per view. --bright is reserved for the final commit inside a flow (submit, approve). Icon buttons are 38px square.

<button class="btn btn--primary">…</button> — modifiers: --primary --bright --secondary --ghost --danger --accent-soft --sm --icon

Tool monograms

Every tool gets a two-letter monogram tile (or its emoji, for live apps) as its identity until custom logos arrive — the tile is the drop-in slot for them.

SL CE VO PO .monogram / .monogram--lg

Filter chips & segmented control

Fixed 32px height, counts inside each chip. Wrap a group in data-pressed-group and components.js handles the exclusive toggling.
